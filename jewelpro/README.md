# JewelPro - Jewellery Store Management System

Complete single-file jewellery store management application.
Handles billing, inventory, GST, karigar management, old gold
exchange, savings schemes, and more. Fully offline, runs in
any modern browser.

---

## Features

### Core
- GST-compliant billing with jewellery-specific rates
- Daily metal rate management (Gold, Silver, Platinum)
- Weight-based pricing with making charges
- Old gold/silver exchange
- Inventory with metal, purity, weight tracking
- Estimate/quotation generation

### Jewellery Specific
- Karigar (artisan) management and work orders
- Repair order tracking
- Gold/Silver savings schemes
- Hallmarking tracking
- Stone details (Diamond, Ruby, etc.)
- Tag printing
- Customer ring/wrist size tracking

### Business
- Customer management with anniversary tracking
- Supplier management
- Purchase recording
- Payment tracking
- Expense management
- GST calculator (jewellery rates)
- GSTIN validator
- HSN code database (jewellery specific)
- Sales, stock, and P&L reports
- 10 achievement badges

### Technical
- Single HTML file, fully offline
- PC-locked license system
- Dark/Light theme
- Command palette (Ctrl+K)
- Export/Import data (JSON)
- CSV exports
- Chart.js analytics
